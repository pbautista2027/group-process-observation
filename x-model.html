The Beach Volleyball Model

The model created by the group after conducting and analyzing the observation.

The Beach Volleyball Model illustrates an analogy between the interactions of different players in the Fortnite game based on the analysis of the two observation sessions that were held. This model may also be applied to other online, communication-heavy, team-based games like role-playing games (RPG), first-person shooter (FPS) games, multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) games, and other Battle Royale games.

This model highlights the interaction between teammates and enemies (receiver/communicator). In beach volleyball, the ball can either be thrown into the enemy team to try to secure a victory or can be passed around by teammates at most three times to prepare and set up for a game-changing blow. Information and messages, in the form of the volleyball (message), can be received at any time whether by a teammate's or by an enemy's hands (channel), which is analogous to the information collection of players in team-based games. After receiving information in the Fortnite game, players usually have a choice to either act on the information immediately or to share the information with teammates while planning the best course of action as a form of feedback (feedback), represented by the option to immediately return the volleyball to the team and to set the ball in preparation to smash in beach volleyball, respectively. This entire process repeats until victory or defeat is achieved (effect), just like what was observed during the Fortnite game sessions.

Lastly, the most noticeable noises in the sand courts of beach volleyball (context) include the sand platforms and the possible gusts of wind (noise). Sand platforms can hinder gameplay as it is more energy-consuming for standing, running, and jumping due to its instability as compared to flat platforms. Some people, like those used to flat surfaces, may have a difficult time moving in sandy areas, while some, like those living near coastal areas, may be more used to these areas and are able to move freely and effectively. This is analogous to the ping barrier in online games, as locations and servers can induce delays in communication for games. In addition to that, the wind that can also hinder the gameplay by affecting how the message is delivered is analogous to the internet connectivity issues in online games. This is because connection errors may misplace, misdirect, or at times, stop the message from being shared, which is similar to how the wind can change the landing location of the volleyball, change the trajectory of the ball flight, and stop the ball if the wind were able to build up enough speed.
