Group Process Observation on In‑Game Communication and Player Behavior in Fortnite

A group process observation examining how verbal and nonverbal communication among teammates shapes real-time decision-making, coordination, and behavior during competitive Fortnite gameplay.

Read the Introduction

Project Overview

Online gaming has enabled people of different ages, communities, and nationalities to connect and communicate with each other while tackling shared challenges and competitions. In fast-paced, competitive team games like Fortnite, swift and efficient communication is essential for coordinated gameplay — professional and high-ranked players rely on simple but effective signals and words to relay information such as enemy position, enemy weapons, and strategy to teammates.

This project observes and analyzes that in-game communication process through the lens of the Interaction and Transactional models of communication, identifying the barriers players face and how communication influences player behavior.
