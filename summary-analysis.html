Synthesis and Analysis

The narrative of metacommunication and the comprehensive analysis of the communication situation.

Synthesis

On August 29, 2026, the scheduled group observation was conducted, observing the interaction of players in the game Fortnite. This was done to look into the unique form of communication that players use during games to continuously share information with each other to stay coordinated while actively discussing strategies and actions that would increase the chances of victory. Two player groups were observed in different sessions, where the data collected was in the form of gameplay and voice-chat recordings. The recordings were captured and edited by Paolo Eli C. Bautista, while Gian Adel Bert S. Afable, Anekein Caldae H. Garcia, Lorenzo S. Mercado, Gieul Samuel Montefalco, and Matteo Andres R. Redillas carefully observed the communication of the players in a Discord call where the gameplay was screen-shared by Paolo Eli C. Bautista.

After starting the Discord voice call to complete the group members for the observation, Paolo Eli C. Bautista orally asked the players in the first session for permission to record their voices, conversations, and gameplay, and then started the game session where most of the interactions took place. While waiting for the game to start, the ping issue was discussed, as delays were observable when connecting people with different devices, connections, and locations, significantly affecting gameplay. In the first session, the players started roaming around the area they selected to find items in preparation for possible encounters with enemies. The players readily updated each other on items they found, such as healing items and weapons that will be necessary during fights. In the absence of combat, the player group talked about their experiences in the game and other game mechanics. However, when enemy players appeared, loud warnings could be heard in the voice chat as players screamed, “Enemy! Enemy!” After the alert, a quick exchange of strategies can be observed as locations, team capabilities, and enemy capabilities were briefly discussed. After repeating these patterns of scouring, alerting, and fighting, the player group in the first session was able to become victorious and became the only remaining team alive by using game skills and other mechanics after brief discussions.

In the second session, other than the ping issue impacting the actual gameplay, the connection issue significantly affected the voice-chat communication, where the voices of players randomly stopped in the middle of speaking. This issue was highlighted as the players needed to adjust and adapt to the communication in the game, speaking louder and saying the same lines repeatedly to give emphasis on the meaning and to ensure that the message would come through. When engaging in combat, especially when urgent action is needed, a noticeable increase in tension and emotions was observed in the voice chat of players to significantly minimize the need for explanation while exclaiming the need for help. Overall, the pattern for the first game was similar to the second. However, a noticeable difference was the level and experience of players, as in the second session, the players were less experienced and were unable to understand specific terms used in player communication, where a player had to explain to another the meaning of some terms. In the end, the players in the second session were also able to become victorious despite the communication and gameplay issues.

Analysis

Verbal and Nonverbal Communication

The verbal communication used was primarily informal, utilizing both normal day-to-day communication and unique player interactions. During normal conversations, especially during the calm phases of the game, the players simply spoke in the English language to casually talk about game mechanics, skill levels, and possible strategies that might be used later in the game. On the other hand, during combat, players utilize vocal emotions and simple phrases to deliver messages to their teammates. An example was when a player repeatedly exclaimed, “Enemy! Enemy!” whenever an enemy was spotted, highlighting the urgency of the situation. Another was when a player asked, “Are they bots?” as “bots,” the short term for “robots,” were likely referring to players with a similar level to robots are run with Artificial Intelligence (AI) in games to fill the needed head count, implying that the player was asking, “Are they weak?” to check for the necessity of help.

There were also notable nonverbal cues observed during the two sessions, especially during combat where time, information, and strategies are all limited and must be used and applied immediately. These nonverbal cues are usually feedback for the shared information, whether it is in the form of response to a strategy or order. An example of this is when discussing strategies for incoming combat, which was specifically seen when the location of the enemies were revealed. When the player calls out “white” or “spray,” it means they have completely broken the enemy's shield, and a few more hits will be enough to eliminate them from the game, increasing the chance of victory. A player saying “I have a shotgun” implies that the player is ready for close-ranged combat, where, as a response, the feedback of the other players was to immediately approach the enemies to decrease the distance.

Communication Noise

In the observation sessions, there were noticeable noises and barriers that caused hindrance in communication, one of which is the ping difference in game. Ping was one of the main barriers that the players were experiencing; it represented the network latency or delay between a player's device and the game server, changing depending on the location of the player's device in relation to the location of the game server. A high ping, especially during combat or when making callouts, can delay the consistent exchange of information, plan discussion, and even the action done as a response to the received information. Other barriers include internet connectivity and mode voice channels, as the voices of players sounded laggy and had bad quality, which caused other players to repeat their messages as their voices were not able to come through the communication channel, and, at times, caused other players to make bad decisions made from misheard messages. If the changing of device location and availing of a better internet connection are not possible, then the course of action the players took was the best solution, simply repeating the message until other players are able to receive and act on the information delivered. Semantic barriers were also observed, especially in the second session where some of the players were unable to understand specific gaming terms that players are generally aware of, causing some of the players to check the knowledge of the inexperienced player in terms of specific gaming terms to make sure that the message will be understood during crucial moments in the game.

Overall, Fortnite players utilize short lines with messages that are easy to respond to and to understand. These messages let players share information while also allowing other players to respond quickly, whether orally or in action, allowing players to communicate efficiently and effectively during the game where time is a crucial factor.
