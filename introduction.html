Background of the group process observation, drawn from the group's final concept paper.

Background of the Observation

Online gaming has enabled people of different ages, communities, and nationalities to connect and to communicate with each other by tackling different challenges and competitions. However, in most competitive team games, swift and efficient communication is an important factor for smooth and well-coordinated gameplay. Fortnite, a battle-royale game where the goal is to survive the longest while eliminating all enemies, is one of the games in which professional and high-ranked players use simple but effective signals and words as forms of communication to immediately deliver different messages to teammates, since variables such as enemy position, enemy weapons, enemy strategy, and game gimmicks can be difficult to act upon by an entire team not aligned in thought (Lovekin, 2018).

In this game, both verbal and nonverbal communication is highlighted, as players send and receive messages back and forth — even in the form of sentences shortened to words, different symbols, and various vocal tones — whenever information that may benefit the team is acquired, allowing each player to be updated on what to do and what not to do at all times. Feedback from other players may take the form of affirmation of receiving the information, confirmation of the message itself, or predictions and recommendations on what to do afterwards. The effect of the communication can be observed frequently, as it is represented by the consequent action of the players after receiving the broadcasted information, whether to act or to avoid a certain situation at all costs.

The communication barrier most prominently observed in this game is the semantic barrier, arising from the possible inability of new and inexperienced players to understand how to send or interpret the forms of communication used by veterans. Other possible barriers in online gaming include technological barriers, due to differences in device capabilities, and language barriers, which could stem from the coalition of players from different nations. Both the Interaction Model and the Transactional Model of communication can be observed in this game: players send messages back and forth to discuss strategies and specific parts of their gameplay, while also continuously receiving information from the game and repeatedly relaying it to other players for updates.

Purpose

The group seeks to examine player discussion by evaluating how various modes of communication influence in-game behavior. Such communication is subject to significant moderation by real-time events and situational possibilities within the game environment. This study also aims to evaluate how these communication modes affect the choices players make during the game, and to identify the communication obstacles they encounter.

Project Details

Location

Virtual observation conducted through the group's private Discord server.

Dates & Times

Saturday, August 29, 2026

Platform

Group members joined a shared Discord call, with one member spectating and screen-sharing the gameplay for the others to observe. The call and livestream were recorded for documentation purposes.

Methods

All group members joined a Discord call. One group member spectated the participants throughout the game and shared their screen with the rest of the group. The call and the live game were recorded for documentation purposes. The conversations will be synthesized by breaking down the recorded conversation into the different elements of communication, as well as the feedback of players in their actual gameplay. This will be explained through the perspective of the Interaction Model of Communication and the Transactional Model of Communication.
